01 · About

TA

I am a Research Coordinator at the Central University Research Center and a permanent lecturer teaching Python, Java, Big Data and Databases. My PhD in Computer Science, completed at the National School of Computer Science (ENSI, Tunisia), focused on distributed deep learning for genomics and real-time execution monitoring on HPC infrastructure.

Between coordinating research teams and teaching, I keep building: recommendation systems, NLP pipelines, and the kind of large-scale training infrastructure that makes AI practical outside the lab. In May and June 2026 I was a Visiting Lecturer at Università Politecnica delle Marche (Italy) through the ITACA exchange project, teaching AI and Data Science and developing new international research collaborations.
